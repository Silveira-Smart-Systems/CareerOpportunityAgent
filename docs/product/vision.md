# Career Opportunity Agent — Product Vision

Status: Draft for review  
Owner: Product / User Zero  
Tracking: #2

## Product statement
Career Opportunity Agent (COA) is an **AI Career Opportunity Intelligence Platform** that helps technology professionals identify, assess, and act on career opportunities aligned with their proven experience, compensation expectations, work-model preferences, and career goals.

COA is a **commercial product in validation**, initially developed and tested by its first real user (**User Zero**). This is a product hypothesis, not a claim of proven demand.

## Problem
Traditional job search and matching can produce large volumes of weakly relevant opportunities. Keyword matches alone do not answer whether an opportunity is commercially viable, compatible with personal constraints, or supported by demonstrated experience.

## Initial customer profile
- Initial user: User Zero, a senior technology professional evaluating remote leadership/architecture opportunities.
- Initial beta audience: 5–10 technology professionals, especially developers, architects, technical leads, and consultants.
- Broader audiences are outside the initial validation scope.

## Core value proposition
COA evaluates **opportunity quality for a specific professional**, not merely skill-keyword similarity. It combines:
1. Verified professional evidence and unknown/unproven capabilities.
2. Technical requirements and seniority.
3. Compensation and contract conditions.
4. Work model, location, and hard constraints.
5. Career alignment and risk.
6. Transparent reasoning and human control.

## Product principles
- Evidence before confidence: never fabricate experience or job requirements.
- Explainable recommendations: show why a score or warning was produced.
- Human agency: COA prepares applications but never submits without explicit human approval.
- Source legitimacy: only authorized integrations or user-supplied opportunity content.
- Privacy by design: minimize personal data shared with AI providers.
- Lean MVP: avoid premature multi-tenant, billing, and microservice complexity.
- GitHub is the single source of truth for requirements, decisions, work, and deliverables.

## Differentiation hypotheses to test
- Separating technical fit from commercial/personal fit is more useful than a single generic match score.
- Evidence-backed gap analysis reduces false confidence.
- Daily curated recommendations save meaningful evaluation time.
- Critical review helps avoid superficially attractive but unsuitable opportunities.

These are hypotheses; market research and User Zero/beta outcomes must confirm or reject them.

## Commercial direction
Potential future subscription SaaS, contingent on usefulness, willingness to pay, legal access to opportunity sources, cost-to-serve, and repeat engagement. Indicative price points (€9/€19/€39 per month) are **unvalidated experiments**, not established pricing.

## Validation path
User Zero dogfooding → measured quality/value → private beta (5–10 professionals) → willingness-to-pay and unit-economics tests → explicit SaaS go/no-go decision.
