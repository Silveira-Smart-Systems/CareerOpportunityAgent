## Material qualification gate
Udemy's official eligibility guidance says affiliates should have an existing active site/social presence and engaged audience, specifying at least 500 unique monthly site visitors for the last 3 months or 500 social followers. This makes Udemy **later-stage application candidate**, not a dependable MVP revenue assumption.

## Open diligence questions
1. Are Portugal-based businesses and the intended international audiences eligible?
2. Do terms permit personalized AI-generated course suggestions and contextual deep links inside a SaaS app?
3. Which products/courses are eligible, what disclosures are required, and are rates region-specific?
4. Are catalog feeds or APIs available to partners, and under what license and refresh constraints?
5. What user tracking, consent, cookie, payout and refund terms apply?
6. How do we independently rank relevance, offer non-affiliate alternatives and prevent sponsored bias?

## Product recommendation
Build the **user-requested, explainable learning-suggestion concept** before relying on partner revenue. Keep the course recommendation algorithm independent of commission. Design commercial disclosure and user controls from the start. Prioritize Pluralsight/Coursera for early eligibility diligence; treat Udemy as a candidate once audience criteria can be met. All actual partner applications/outreach, tracking, integrations, code, merge and deploy require separate approval.

This is not a forecast, legal opinion or a representation of acceptance by any program. Terms may change.
